PRIVACY AND SECURITY

Your life is yours alone

My Life is built so your information stays with you. Here is the plain version.

🔐

On your phone first, backed up to your account.

Your data is encrypted on your phone. When you are signed in, a private copy is saved to your account so you can use it on another phone. Each account can only read its own data, and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

What we storeOnly what you enter: notes, events, finance entries, records and habits. App-use stats stay on your device for 30 days.
What we never doNo ads, no tracking across other apps, no selling your data. Location is only used for prayer times.
LocationUsed only to work out prayer times. It is not saved or sent anywhere, except to the prayer-time service to look up your zone.
App lockOptional PIN or fingerprint. Your PIN is kept on your phone in scrambled form. Nobody can see it or reset it for you.
Your controlExport everything to Excel, PDF, CSV or a backup file, reset all your data, or delete your account, any time from Settings.
Delete anytimeDelete your account in the app and everything saved online is removed too.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Quick answers before you install.

Does My Life have ads?

No. There are no ads and we do not sell your data.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You register once in the app with your name, email and a password. It lets your data follow you to a new phone. After registering, you unlock the app with an activation code from the developer.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, almost everything works offline. Changes are saved on your phone first and synced to your account automatically when you are online. Internet is also used for things like looking up your prayer zone or refreshing the list of Malaysian holidays.

What if I change or lose my phone?

Install My Life on the new phone and sign in with the same email and password. Your data comes back. Receipt photos are not included, so keep a backup file from Settings, Export too.

Why does Android say it is an unknown app?

The app is installed from a file instead of the Play Store, so Android asks you to allow it once. Download only from the official link on this page.

Are the prayer times accurate?

They follow JAKIM zones for Malaysia. Please confirm with your local authority where it matters.

Is the Assistant real AI?

It is a built-in helper that answers from your own data on your phone. Your questions are not sent to any server or AI service.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings and reset your data, or remove the app from your phone.

How do I report a problem or share an idea?

Use “Report a problem” in Settings, or write to the contact address below.

GET THE APP

Install on your Android phone

It takes about a minute. Follow the steps one by one.

1
Open the download page

Tap the green “Download for Android” button. It opens the My Life release page on GitHub.

Download for Android
2
Download the APK file

Scroll to “Assets” on that page and tap the file that ends with .apk. Your phone downloads it. If your browser warns that this type of file can harm your device, tap “Download anyway”, as long as you came from this page.

3
Open the file

When the download finishes, tap “Open” in the notification, or open the file from the Downloads folder in your Files app.

4
Allow the install

If Android says it cannot install apps from this source, tap “Settings”, switch on “Allow from this source”, then go back and tap “Install”. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap “More details”, then “Install anyway”.

5
Open My Life

Tap “Open”. When asked, allow notifications and location so prayer times and reminders can work. You can change these later in Settings.

6
Create your account

On first open you will see the sign-in page. Tap register, enter your name, email and a password, then you land on Home. Your name is shown on your Home screen, and you can change it later in Settings.

Updating the appFirst save a backup from Settings, Export. Then come back to this page, download the newest APK and open it. If Android will not install it over the old one, remove the old app, install the new one and restore your backup.
Install does not start?Make sure the APK finished downloading, there is enough free space, and “Allow from this source” is on for the browser or Files app you used.
Safe to install?Only download from the link on this page. Android shows the same warning for any app installed outside the Play Store.
