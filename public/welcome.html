PRIVACY AND SECURITY

Your life is yours alone

My Life 1.0 is built so your information stays with you. Here is the plain version.

🔐

On your phone first, backed up to your account.

Your data is encrypted on your phone. When you are signed in, a private copy is saved to your account so you can use it on another phone. Each account can only read its own data (apart from the Family Planner space you share), and you can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

What we storeOnly what you enter: notes, events, finance entries, records, habits and, if you use Family Planner, the family space you share. App-use stats stay on your device for 30 days.
What we never doNo ads, no tracking across other apps, no selling your data. Location is only used for prayer times.
LocationUsed only to find your prayer zone. It is not saved; it is sent to the prayer-time service only to look up your zone.
App lockOptional PIN or fingerprint. Your PIN is kept on your phone in scrambled form. Nobody can see it or reset it for you.
SubscriptionsYour subscription list is saved to your account, including each one's status and the payment type you note. No card numbers are ever asked for or kept. Logo images stay on your phone, and bills you scan are read on your phone and not kept. Bill text goes to the online assistant only if you turn on Online AI and ask it to read again. My Life 1.0 cannot cancel anything for you.
My AccountsThe account names and balances you type are saved to your account like your other finance entries. My Life 1.0 never connects to your bank and never asks for a login or card number.
Your controlExport everything, including Documents and Personal Collection, to Excel, PDF, CSV or a backup file, reset all your data, or delete your account, any time from Settings.
Delete anytimeDelete your account in the app and everything saved online is removed too.

TERMS OF USE

The short version

Fair and simple. The full text is always in the app under Settings.

My Life 1.0 is a personal organiser for your own use. Keep your phone and your PIN private. You are responsible for what is done on your device.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Quick answers before you install.

Does My Life 1.0 have ads?

No. There are no ads and we do not sell your data.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Register once with your name, email and a password so your data follows you to a new phone. Then unlock the app with an activation code from the developer.

Can my family use it together?

Yes. Family Planner shares tasks, chores, a shopping list, a menu and a calendar. The owner shares an invite code and each person needs their own account. Children without a phone can be added as profiles.

Can I use it outside Malaysia?

Yes. Pick your country and currency in Settings, and your public holidays and money symbol follow it. Prayer times follow JAKIM zones, so they are accurate in Malaysia only.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, almost everything. Changes are saved on your phone first and synced when you are online. Internet is used for things like your prayer zone and the holiday list.

What if I change or lose my phone?

Install My Life 1.0 on the new phone and sign in with the same email and password, and your data comes back. Photos (receipts, Documents, Personal Collection) stay on your phone, so keep a Backup (JSON) from Settings, Export to move them.

Why does Android say it is an unknown app?

The app is installed from a file instead of the Play Store, so Android asks you to allow it once. Download only from the official link on this page.

Are the prayer times accurate?

They follow JAKIM zones, so they are only accurate in Malaysia. Outside Malaysia the times may be wrong. Please confirm with your local authority where it matters.

Is the Assistant real AI?

It starts as a built-in helper that answers from your data on your phone, and nothing is sent anywhere. For other questions, turn on AI. Smart chat runs on your phone with no internet. Online AI is optional, off by default, and sends your question through our server to an AI service. AI can be wrong, so check important facts.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings and reset your data, or remove the app from your phone.

How do I report a problem or share an idea?

Use “Report a problem” in Settings, or write to the contact address below.

GET THE APP

Install on your Android phone

It takes about a minute. Follow the steps one by one.

1
Open the download page

Tap the green “Download for Android” button. It opens the My Life 1.0 release page on GitHub.

Download for Android
2
Download the APK file

Scroll to “Assets” and tap the file ending in .apk. If your browser warns about this file type, tap “Download anyway”.

3
Open the file

Tap “Open” in the download notification, or open the file from your Files app.

4
Allow the install

If Android blocks the install, tap “Settings”, switch on “Allow from this source”, then tap “Install”. If Play Protect warns, tap “More details”, then “Install anyway”.

5
Open My Life 1.0

Tap “Open” and allow notifications so reminders work. You can change this later in Settings.

6
Create your account

Tap register, enter your name, email and a password, and you land on Home. You can change your name later in Settings.

Updating the appSave a backup from Settings, Export first. Then download the newest APK from this page and open it. If Android will not install it over the old one, remove the old app, install the new one and restore your backup.
Install does not start?Check that the APK finished downloading, you have free space, and “Allow from this source” is on.
Safe to install?Only download from the link on this page. Android shows the same warning for any app installed outside the Play Store.
