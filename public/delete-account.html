My Life 1.0Privacy

Delete your account

You can delete your My Life 1.0 account and all the data saved with it at any time, from inside the app.

How to delete it in the app

It takes less than a minute.

1

Open Settings

In My Life 1.0, open the Settings tab and find the Account and data section.

2

Tap Delete account

Tap the red Delete account button.

3

Confirm

Type DELETE and enter your password, then confirm. Your account is removed straight away.

What is deleted

Everything is removed from our server at once.

Deleted

Your account and sign-in, and the copy of your data saved online: planner, notes, finance, debts and savings, vehicle records, habits, your name and your settings.

Kept

Nothing about you is kept after deletion. There is no retention period and no backup of your data. Data that exists only on your phone stays there until you remove the app or reset it in Settings.

Want to keep only some data?

You do not have to delete the whole account.

Reset your data

In Settings, Reset everything erases all your data and the online copy, and keeps your account. You can also delete single items inside each part of the app.

Cannot open the app?

Ask us and we will do it for you.

Email us to delete your account

Send the request from the email address you signed up with. We delete the account and its data within 7 days and reply to confirm.
