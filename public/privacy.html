What we store

Your account (name, email and a password that we only keep in scrambled form), and what you enter yourself: notes, events, finance entries, records and habits. Stats about how long you use the app are kept on your device for 30 days. For security and to protect licences, the app owner can see how many devices are signed in to each account and when the account was last active. This does not include your notes, finances or other personal data. The app itself does not store IP addresses or device names, but the sign-in service we use (Supabase) may keep technical records, such as an IP address, for security.

2

Where it lives

Your data lives first on your phone, encrypted there. When you are signed in, a copy is also saved to your account on our server (Supabase, hosted in Singapore) so you can use it on other phones and get it back if you lose this one. Each account can only read its own data, plus the shared Family Planner space of the family it belongs to. Receipt photos stay on the phone and are not uploaded. Your subscription list (names, prices, dates) and your accounts in My Finance are saved to your account too, but the logo images you pick stay on the phone only. When you scan a bill, the text is read on your phone and the picture is not kept; only if you tap Read with AI is that text, never the picture, sent to the online assistant. The details you type in Documents and Personal Collection are saved to your account like your other data, but their photos stay on the phone only. The one exception is a private photo backup that is switched on for a single account by the app's owner: those photos are kept in a private storage folder that only that account can open.

3

Family Planner

If you use Family Planner, what you add there is saved on our server in a space shared by your family: the family name, members' names and colours, birthdays you add, tasks, chores and reward points, the shopping list, menu, wishes and activities. Every member can see and change it. This is the one place where data is shared between accounts, and only people who joined with your family code can see it. The owner can add profiles for people without an account, such as children; we only keep a name, a colour and, if you add it, a birthday for them. Deleted items stay in Recently deleted for 7 days. Anyone can leave a family. The owner can remove members or delete the family, which deletes everything in it. If the owner deletes their account, the family and its data are deleted too.

4

What we never do

We do not show ads, track you across other apps or sell your data. We only send data to the services the app needs in order to work, listed under Other services, and only what each one needs. The server stores your data so the app can sync it; it is not end-to-end encrypted, so the developer could technically access it, but does not read it.

5

Location

Prayer times use your location only to work out the times. Your coordinates are sent to the prayer-time service (waktusolat.app) to look up your zone. The last place found is kept on your phone only, so times can show without asking again, and it is not saved to your account. You can choose your zone by hand instead.

6

Steps and health

If you allow it, My Life reads your step count from your phone's step sensor or Health Connect to fill in your daily goal. Steps are kept with your habit data: saved on your phone and, when you are signed in, in your online copy. They are not used for anything else. If you turn on Pulse from my smart watch in the Heart log, My Life also reads your heart rate from Health Connect (only after you allow it). Your phone shares nothing with My Life until you do, and My Life never talks to the watch itself. One reading every 6 hours is added to your Heart log and kept like your other habit data. For the night, the lowest reading is kept, and My Life 1.0 compares it with your own usual night pulse to improve the Energy estimate. You can turn it off, delete any reading, and take away the permission in Health Connect at any time. It is a guide only, not medical advice.

7

Medicine reminders

If you add medicines in Habit and Health, the names, doses and times you type, and what you tick as taken, are kept with your habit data: encrypted on your phone and, when you are signed in, in your online copy. They are used only to remind you and to show which doses you missed, worked out on your phone. My Life does not give medical or dose advice. Relax sessions (which exercise, how long and when) are kept in the same way. If you turn on Cycle tracking, the days you mark as a period and the symptoms you tick are kept in the same way. They are used only to show your own record and estimates, never for advertising, and My Life does not give medical advice. If you log your pulse or blood pressure, the numbers you type are kept in the same way and used only to show your own record. My Life does not measure them, judge them or give medical advice. The reminder shows the medicine name on your lock screen, so turn Medicine reminders off in Settings if you prefer.

8

Quran Audio

Recitations you download are saved only on your phone, in the app's own storage, and you can delete them any time in Quran Audio, Downloads. They are not uploaded and not part of your online copy. The reciter you picked, the Wi-Fi setting and the place where you stopped listening are also kept on your phone only. Removing the app removes them.

9

Personal Collection

The things you add in Personal Collection, such as names, prices, dates, notes, who you lent to or borrowed from, and your life lessons, are saved on your phone and in your account backup like your other data. Photos are kept on your phone only and are never uploaded. Reminders are made on your phone. If you save a backup file or an export from Settings, what you choose goes into that file, which stays with you.

10

Documents and scanning

The documents you add in My Record, such as names, numbers and expiry dates, are saved on your phone and in your account backup like your other data. The photos are kept on your phone only and are never uploaded. When you tap Scan with camera, My Life 1.0 asks for camera permission only then. The page is found, straightened and cleaned up on your phone by the Google document scanner, which is part of Google Play services on your phone. The words in the photo are read on your phone by the phone itself, and the photo is not sent anywhere. If you save a backup file or an export from Settings, the documents you choose go into that file, which stays with you. Reminders are made on your phone.

11

Scans in Documents

The Scans part of Documents lets you scan papers into a PDF. The pages are found, straightened and cleaned on your phone by the Google document scanner, which is part of Google Play services, and the filters you choose are applied there. Camera permission is asked only when you tap Scan pages. The scanned pages and their names stay on your phone only: they are not synced, not uploaded and not in your account backup. The PDF is made on your phone and only you decide where to share it.

12

Maintenance tasks

The tasks you add in My Record, their dates, km limits and any cost you type are kept on your phone and in your account backup like your other vehicle records. Reminders are made on your phone and nothing extra is sent anywhere.

13

Your Memories

Your Memories with My Life 1.0 is worked out on your phone from data you already saved, such as notes, tasks, plans, health logs and records. The only extra things saved are the memories you chose to hide, the date each badge was first unlocked, and whether the Dashboard card is on. They are backed up with your other data and are removed when you reset your data or delete your account. Nothing is shared with other people.

14

Sleep timer

When you tap Going to sleep, the start time is kept on your phone and a notification is shown. When you tap I'm awake, the hours are saved in your Habit and Health data like any other entry. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

15

My Life Live (talking to the Assistant)

The microphone button in the Assistant listens only after you tap it and allow the microphone, and it stops when you stop talking or close the voice screen. Your voice is turned into text by the speech recognition service of your phone (on most Android phones this is Google's speech service, which may send the audio to its servers unless an offline language is installed on the phone). My Life 1.0 does not record or keep your voice and does not send it to our server. The words are then handled like anything you type to the Assistant. The Assistant reads its answers aloud with your phone's own voice. When you chat by voice, the AI on your phone answers first if it is downloaded. If you turned on Online AI and the AI on your phone is not ready, your words and your last few messages are sent over the internet to get the answer. When you talk in another language, your words are translated on your phone with the language pack, and the answer is translated back. The language packs are downloaded once from Google. Your chosen language, voice, tone and speed are kept only on your phone.

16

Hafazan Check

Hafazan Check uses the microphone only while you recite, after you allow it. Your voice is turned into text on your phone by a Quran model that runs there. The audio is not recorded to a file, not uploaded and not sent to any server, and the app works with the result as text only. The model (about 57 MB) is downloaded once from GitHub, and you can delete it in Hafazan Check. Your chosen surah, verses and settings are kept on your phone only.

17

Islamic Hub activity

Which days you opened a surah or zikir, shown in Your week, is kept on your phone only and is not part of your online copy. It keeps the last 45 days. The Hijri date is read from JAKIM (e-solat.gov.my) when you are online, with no personal data sent, and the difference to the built-in calendar is kept on your phone.

18

Notes and reminders

The notes you write, their reminder times and any shopping list you keep in Notes are kept with your other data: encrypted on your phone and, when you are signed in, in your online copy. A reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone, so it also works offline, and it shows the note's title on your lock screen. If you tell the Assistant to remind you, it saves the reminder in the same way.

19

AI answers (optional)

The Assistant answers from your own data first. For other questions you can turn on AI, and nothing is sent until you do. Online AI sends the question you type, and the language you use, over the internet through our server to an AI service (OpenRouter), which passes it to an AI model provider to write the answer. Our server only counts how many questions each account asks per day to enforce a limit. It does not save your questions or the answers, but the AI service and its model providers may handle them under their own policies, so do not type passwords, ID numbers or other secrets. You must be signed in to use it. Smart chat runs on your phone: the model is downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), and after that the questions and answers stay on your phone and are not sent anywhere. Smart chat answers can be wrong; check important facts.

20

Chat in your language

Translating your messages is done on your phone with Google ML Kit. The first time you pick a language, the language pack is downloaded from Google. Your messages are not sent to Google or to us for translation.

21

How you feel

If you use Feeling unwell? in the Quick log, the symptom you pick or type, such as headache or tired, is kept with your habit data in the same way. The energy estimate is worked out on your phone from what you logged. They are used only to show your own record and are never used for advertising. My Life does not diagnose or give medical advice.

22

Other services

To work, the app contacts these services: waktusolat.app and e-solat.gov.my for prayer times and the Hijri date (your location is sent to waktusolat.app, see Location); date.nager.at for Malaysian public holidays (no personal data is sent); and Supabase for sign-in and sync. If you use AI: openrouter.ai (through our server) for Online AI and huggingface.co to download Smart chat models. For language chat, Google downloads the language pack. In the GitHub edition, GitHub is contacted when you tap Check for updates. GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) is also contacted once to download the Hafazan Check model. As with any internet request, each service can see your device's IP address. Quran Audio streams and downloads recitations from alquran.cloud (cdn.islamic.network). Your phone contacts them when you play or save audio, and, as with any web request, they can see your IP address. We send them nothing else.

23

App lock

If you turn on App lock, your PIN is stored only on your phone in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot see it or reset it for you.

24

Your control

You can export everything at any time from Settings (you choose which modules go in the file, including ones you turned off), reset all your data (this also erases the online copy), or delete your account in Settings, which permanently removes your account and everything saved online. If you own a family, deleting your account also deletes the family. Removing the app from your phone removes only the data on that phone.
