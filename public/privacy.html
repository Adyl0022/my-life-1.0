Privacy Policy

What we store and why.

What we storeWhere it livesWhat we never doLocationSteps and healthOther servicesApp lockYour controlContact
1

What we store

Your account (name, email and a password we keep only in scrambled form), and what you enter yourself: notes, events, finance entries, records and habits. Stats about how long you use the app are kept on your device for 30 days. For security and to protect licences, the app owner can see how many devices are signed in to each account and when the account was last active. This does not include your notes, finances or other personal data. The app itself does not store IP addresses or device names, but the sign-in service we use (Supabase) may keep technical records, such as an IP address, for security.

2

Where it lives

Your data lives first on your phone, encrypted there. When you are signed in, a copy is also saved to your account on our server (Supabase, hosted in Singapore) so you can use it on other phones and get it back if you lose this one. Each account can only read its own data. Receipt photos stay on the phone and are not uploaded.

3

What we never do

We do not show ads, track you across other apps or sell your data. We only send data to the services the app needs in order to work, listed under Other services, and only what each one needs. The server stores your data so the app can sync it; it is not end-to-end encrypted, so the developer could technically access it, but does not read it.

4

Location

Prayer times use your location only to work out the times. Your coordinates are sent to the prayer-time service (waktusolat.app) to look up your zone. The last place found is kept on your phone only, so times can show without asking again, and it is not saved to your account. You can choose your zone by hand instead.

5

Steps and health

If you allow it, My Life reads your step count from your phone's step sensor or Health Connect to fill in your daily goal. Steps are kept with your habit data: saved on your phone and, when you are signed in, in your online copy. They are not used for anything else.

6

Other services

To work, the app contacts these services: waktusolat.app and e-solat.gov.my for prayer times (your location is sent to waktusolat.app, see Location); date.nager.at for Malaysian public holidays (no personal data is sent); and Supabase for sign-in and sync. In the GitHub edition, GitHub is contacted when you tap Check for updates. As with any internet request, each service can see your device's IP address.

7

App lock

If you turn on App lock, your PIN is stored only on your phone in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot see it or reset it for you.

8

Your control

You can export everything at any time from Settings, reset all your data (this also erases the online copy), or delete your account in Settings, which permanently removes your account and everything saved online. Removing the app from your phone removes only the data on that phone.

9

Contact

Questions about privacy go to the developer, Adli Syahmi.
