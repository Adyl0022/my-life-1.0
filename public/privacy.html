What we store

Your account (name, email and a password kept only in scrambled form) and what you enter: notes, events, finance entries, records and habits. Usage stats stay on your device for 30 days. For security and licences, the app owner can see how many devices are signed in to each account and when it was last active, never your notes, finances or other personal data. The app does not store IP addresses or device names, but our sign-in service (Supabase) may keep technical records such as an IP address for security.

2

Where it lives

Your data lives first on your phone, encrypted. When you are signed in, a copy is saved to your account on our server (Supabase, Singapore) so you can use other phones and recover it if you lose this one. Each account can only read its own data, plus its own family's Family Planner. Photos stay on your phone only: receipt photos, subscription and account logos, and photos in Documents and Personal Collection. Their text details are saved to your account. When you scan a bill, the text is read on your phone and the picture is not kept; only if you tap Read with AI is that text (never the picture) sent to the online assistant. One exception: a private photo backup the app owner can switch on for a single account, kept in a private folder only that account can open.

3

Family Planner

Family Planner data is saved on our server in a space shared by your family: family name, members' names and colours, birthdays you add, tasks, chores, reward points, shopping list, menu, wishes and activities. Every member can see and change it, and only people with your family code can join. This is the only place data is shared between accounts. The owner can add profiles for people without an account, such as children (a name, a colour and an optional birthday). Deleted items stay in Recently deleted for 7 days. Anyone can leave. The owner can remove members or delete the family, which deletes everything in it, and deleting the owner's account deletes the family too.

4

What we never do

We do not show ads, track you across other apps or sell your data. We send data only to the services the app needs, listed under Other services, and only what each one needs. Your data on the server is not end-to-end encrypted, so the developer could technically access it, but does not read it.

5

Location

Prayer times use your location only to find your zone: your coordinates go to the prayer-time service (waktusolat.app). The last place found stays on your phone only, not in your account. You can choose your zone by hand instead.

6

Steps and health

If you allow it, My Life 1.0 reads your steps from your phone's step sensor or Health Connect for your daily goal. Steps are kept with your habit data (on your phone and, when signed in, your online copy) and used for nothing else. If you turn on Pulse from my smart watch, it also reads your heart rate from Health Connect, only after you allow it. It never talks to the watch itself. One reading every 6 hours is added to your Heart log, and the lowest night reading is compared with your usual night pulse to improve the Energy estimate. You can turn it off, delete any reading, or remove the permission at any time. It is a guide only, not medical advice.

7

Medicine reminders

Medicine names, doses, times and what you tick as taken, plus Relax sessions, Cycle tracking days and symptoms, and any pulse or blood pressure you type, are kept with your habit data: encrypted on your phone and, when signed in, in your online copy. They are used only to remind you and to show your own record and estimates, worked out on your phone, never for advertising. My Life 1.0 does not measure, judge or give medical or dose advice. A reminder shows the medicine name on your lock screen, so turn Medicine reminders off in Settings if you prefer.

8

Quran Audio

Downloaded recitations are saved only on your phone, in the app's own storage. Delete them any time in Quran Audio, Downloads. They are not uploaded or part of your online copy. Your reciter, Wi-Fi setting and listening position also stay on your phone only.

9

Personal Collection

What you add in Personal Collection (names, prices, dates, notes, who you lent to or borrowed from, life lessons) is saved on your phone and in your account backup. Photos stay on your phone only and are never uploaded. Reminders are made on your phone. A backup file or export you save from Settings stays with you.

10

Documents and scanning

Details you add in My Record Documents (names, numbers, expiry dates) are saved on your phone and in your account backup. Photos stay on your phone only. Scan with camera asks for camera permission only when you tap it. The Google document scanner (part of Google Play services) straightens and cleans the page on your phone, and your phone reads the words itself. The photo is not sent anywhere. A backup file or export stays with you. Reminders are made on your phone.

11

Spending from a sentence

If the Assistant does not understand a spending sentence with its normal rules, the AI model on your phone (if downloaded) reads it and fills in the amount, category and account. This happens only on your phone, not on the internet or Online AI. Nothing is saved until you confirm. The account link syncs like your other spending.

12

Scans in Documents

The Scans part turns papers into a PDF. The Google document scanner (part of Google Play services) finds, straightens and cleans the pages on your phone. Camera permission is asked only when you tap Scan pages. Scanned pages and their names stay on your phone only: not synced, not uploaded and not in your account backup. You decide where to share the PDF.

13

Maintenance tasks

Tasks you add in My Record, with their dates, km limits and costs, are kept on your phone and in your account backup like your other vehicle records. Reminders are made on your phone and nothing extra is sent.

14

Your Memories

Your Memories with My Life 1.0 is worked out on your phone from data you already saved. The only extra things saved are memories you hid, the date each badge unlocked and whether the Dashboard card is on. They are backed up with your other data and removed when you reset your data or delete your account. Nothing is shared with others.

15

Sleep timer

Going to sleep keeps the start time on your phone and shows a notification. I'm awake saves the hours in your Habit and Health data like any entry. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

16

Experiments

Your experiments, notes and History are kept with your Habit and Health data: encrypted on your phone and, when signed in, in your online copy. The optional 8:30 pm reminder is scheduled on your phone. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share, which sends only the short summary you choose.

17

Talking to My Life Assistant

The microphone listens only after you tap it and allow the microphone, and stops when you stop talking or close the voice screen. Your phone's speech recognition turns your voice into text (on most Android phones this is Google's service, which may send audio to its servers unless an offline language is installed). My Life 1.0 does not record or keep your voice or send it to our server. The words are then handled like anything you type. Answers are read aloud with your phone's own voice. By voice, the AI on your phone answers first if downloaded. If you turned on Online AI and the phone AI is not ready, your words and last few messages are sent over the internet to get the answer. In another language, your words are translated on your phone with a language pack downloaded once from Google. Your language, voice, tone and speed stay on your phone only.

18

Assistant and your own data

To answer questions about you, the AI on your phone gets a short summary of your saved data (such as spending, commitments, goals, habits, tasks and events). It is made on your phone each time, used only for that answer and not saved. It is never sent to Online AI, which receives only the question you type or say, plus your last few messages in a voice chat. Sums are worked out on your phone without AI.

19

Hafazan Check

Hafazan Check uses the microphone only while you recite, after you allow it. A Quran model on your phone turns your voice into text. The audio is not recorded, uploaded or sent to any server. The model (about 57 MB) is downloaded once from GitHub and can be deleted in Hafazan Check. Your surah, verses and settings stay on your phone only.

20

Islamic Hub activity

The days you opened a surah or zikir, shown in Your week, stay on your phone only (last 45 days) and are not in your online copy. The Hijri date is read from JAKIM (e-solat.gov.my) when you are online, with no personal data sent.

21

Notes and reminders

Notes, reminder times and any shopping list in Notes are kept with your other data: encrypted on your phone and, when signed in, in your online copy. A reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone, so it works offline and shows the note's title on your lock screen. Reminders made through the Assistant are saved the same way.

22

AI answers (optional)

The Assistant answers from your own data first. Nothing goes to AI until you turn it on. Online AI sends the question you type, and your language, through our server to an AI service (OpenRouter), which passes it to a model provider. Our server only counts questions per account each day for a limit and does not save questions or answers, but the AI service and its providers may handle them under their own policies, so do not type passwords, ID numbers or other secrets. You must be signed in. Smart chat runs on your phone: its model is downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), then questions and answers stay on your phone. Smart chat can be wrong, so check important facts.

23

Chat in your language

Translation runs on your phone with Google ML Kit. The language pack is downloaded from Google the first time you pick a language. Your messages are not sent to Google or to us.

24

How you feel

In Feeling unwell? the symptom you pick or type is kept with your habit data. The energy estimate is worked out on your phone from what you logged. They are used only to show your own record, never for advertising. My Life 1.0 does not diagnose or give medical advice.

25

Other services

The app contacts: waktusolat.app and e-solat.gov.my (prayer times and Hijri date; your location goes to waktusolat.app, see Location); date.nager.at (public holidays, no personal data); Supabase (sign-in and sync); alquran.cloud (cdn.islamic.network) for Quran Audio; and GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) once for the Hafazan Check model, and for Check for updates in the GitHub edition. If you use AI: openrouter.ai (through our server) for Online AI, huggingface.co for Smart chat models, and Google for language packs. As with any internet request, each service can see your device's IP address. We send them nothing else.

26

App lock

Your App lock PIN is stored only on your phone in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot see or reset it.

27

Your control

In Settings you can export everything (choose which modules, including ones you turned off), reset all your data (this also erases the online copy), or delete your account, which permanently removes it and everything saved online. If you own a family, it is deleted too. Removing the app removes only the data on that phone.
